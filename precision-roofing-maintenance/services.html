Core services

Five core services.

All centred on tiled roofs. Each one starts the same way: a call or an email to Adam.

  • 01

    Roof repairs

    Repairs to tiled roofs. If something on your roof needs fixing, call or email Adam. He’ll take a look and explain clearly what’s required before any work is done.

    • Tell Adam what you’ve noticed

    Call Adam about this
  • 02

    Repointing

    Pointing is the mortar finish that holds ridge and hip capping in place on a tiled roof. Repointing is redoing that finish where it has worn or cracked.

    • Ridge capping
    • Hip capping
    Call Adam about this
  • 03

    Roof tile replacement

    Replacing roof tiles that are cracked, broken or missing.

    • Cracked tiles
    • Broken tiles
    • Missing tiles
    Call Adam about this
  • 04

    General roof maintenance

    General maintenance to help keep a tiled roof in good order. Call or email to talk through what your roof needs.

    Call Adam about this
  • 05

    Tiled roofing

    Tiled roofs are the core of Adam’s work — the repairs, repointing and tile replacement above all happen on tiled roofs.
